1 · Research question and objectives

The Assembled Periphery

Under which conditions are industrial products from elsewhere assembled with the vernacular and the terrain of the high Alps, and what do these assemblies tell us about supply, autarky and the image of the Alps today?

  • Focus: the assembly itself, where new materials meet old structures and the terrain. Parts from elsewhere have always arrived, such as iron plates and sheet metal since the late 19th century. What is new: which parts, and how fast.
  • Conditions: infrastructure and “progress”, a changing climate, money, and the availability of things such as small solar panels.
  • Reading: the assemblies as an architectural reference, and what happens to what is left behind.
  • Voices: conversations along the way, and with those who build, including the researchers behind a measuring station.

2 · Scope and limitations

Where the work looks, and where it stops

  • Field: the vernacular of the present, infrastructure in the Alps, reading and assembling (Ungers, Lévi-Strauss).
  • Territory: side valleys of the Valais with retreating glaciers, or a selection of them. So far Gemmi and Lämmeren, Val de Bagnes. Which case is central in the end, and whether one is needed, remains open.
  • Periphery: situations, places and actors at the margin, defined by their relation to a distant centre. The work moves them into the centre.
  • Boundary: not the large structures, but the moments of assembly. Situations, objects and atmospheres.

4 · Methodology and approach

Walking, reading, mapping

  • Walking: fieldtrips on foot, reached by train and bus, read from top to bottom.
  • Selection: a find counts when it deviates from its neighbours, from similar places or from earlier states.
  • Station sheets: photograph, draw, describe. Primary source, secondary source and analysis are kept apart.
  • Mapping: terrain as point cloud (swissALTI3D), glacier inventories 1850 to 2016 (GLAMOS), land use and infrastructure.
  • Reading: Ungers for reading, Lévi-Strauss for assembling, the Valais farmhouse research, Fest Flüssig Biotisch, Labor der Extreme.
